About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate focusing on the intersection of deep learning and synthetic biology. My work includes developing generative models for mRNA sequence design, circular RNA analysis, and computational modeling of translation efficiency.

Selected Projects

mRNAdesigner: Pretrained mRNA Language Model

Pretrained from scratch using bacterial sequences across all domains of life for mRNA generation and optimization.
